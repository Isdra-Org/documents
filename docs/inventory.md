# Inventory of ISDRA official documents

Compiled 2026-10-08 from three sources:

- **isdra.org**: the documents linked from the About, Rules, Programs and Policies pages (20 files, all under
  `https://isdra.org/assets/documents/`).
- **The legacy site's Resources page** (`http://isdraorg000.web704.discountasp.net/Resources_Directory/`, 44 files
  under `/_PDFfiles/`). Many are byte-identical to the current files; the rest are older versions or documents the
  new site doesn't link.
- **The website repo's `src/assets/documents/`** (`Isdra-Org/isdra`), which also holds older versions under
  date-stamped names, including one found nowhere else (Dryland 2025).
- The adult points program's Word originals (2019 and 2023), from ISDRA's points-program files.

"In force" means linked from isdra.org on 2026-10-08. Dates are as printed on the document ("Amended July 2026");
where nothing is printed, the PDF's creation date is given in brackets.

Document names are the titles printed on the documents (agreed with John, 2026-10-08). Five repo folders keep their
earlier, shorter names: `governance/Constitution and By-Laws`, `policies/Committee Chair Appointment Policy`,
`sanctioning/Junior Sanctioning Requirements and Standards`, `rules/Distance Race Rules` and
`policies/Policy and Procedure for New Proposals`.
